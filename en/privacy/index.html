Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Span (named 束の間 in Japanese and 寸晷 in Chinese; "the app") is made by Bingyi Wang, an independent developer. Our principle: your photos and their sound are processed on your iPhone, and nothing is sent anywhere without your permission.

In short

The current version of the app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party components, and the app itself makes no network requests.

Photos and sound

  • The app asks for access to your photo library so it can pick Live Photos at random, play their sound, and delete the photos you choose to delete.
  • Photos, videos and sound are read and played only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded, and the developer cannot see them.
  • If you allow access to selected photos only, the app sees only those.
  • Every deletion goes through the system confirmation dialog and needs your tap to confirm. Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them.
  • If a photo's original is stored in iCloud, the system downloads it from iCloud when it plays. This is an Apple system service covered by Apple's privacy policy; the app receives none of that data.

Data kept on your iPhone

To work, the app keeps the following inside the app on your iPhone:

  • Which photos you have seen and which you have saved (the sound album)
  • Statistics for each session, and the feeling and how-moved rating you picked
  • Sound categories detected on the device
  • Your settings

This data stays on your iPhone. It is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app.

Location

If a photo contains the coordinates where it was taken, the app turns them into a city name using city data built into the app. This happens on your iPhone without going online, and the app does not ask for location permission.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details, only whether this device has an active purchase.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated as the app changes. If we add a feature that needs to send content off your iPhone (for example, AI generation), we will:

  • Tell you in the app in advance and update this policy.
  • Before you first use that feature, explain what would be sent, to whom and how long it is kept, and ask for your separate consent.
  • Keep the rest of the app fully usable if you say no.

The effective date on this page is updated with every change.
