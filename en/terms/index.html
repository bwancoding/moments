Free and paid

  • For free, you can start one session a day. Deleting, saving and the sound album are always free.
  • A purchase removes the daily limit. There are two options with the same benefits:
    • Yearly subscription: renews automatically every year.
    • Lifetime: pay once, keep it forever.

Subscription details

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the next year within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. Please request them from Apple.
  • On a new iPhone or after reinstalling the app, tap Restore purchases in the app's settings.

Deleting photos

The app deletes only the photos you mark during a session and then confirm in the system dialog. Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where the system usually keeps them for 30 days and you can recover them. Please check the photos before you confirm.
