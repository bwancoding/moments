I can't hear anything

When your iPhone is on silent, Span stays silent too. Switch to "Always Play" in Span's Settings to hear sound even on silent.

Photos take a while to load

Photos stored in iCloud Photos are downloaded by iOS before they play. Try again somewhere with a good connection.

Why does Span need access to all photos?

So it can pick at random from your whole library. You can change access in Settings → Apps → Span → Photos. Your photos and sounds are processed on your iPhone and never leave it without your OK.

How do I get a deleted photo back?

Open Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days.

My purchase is gone after switching phones

Tap "Restore purchases" in Span's Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (Report a Problem).
